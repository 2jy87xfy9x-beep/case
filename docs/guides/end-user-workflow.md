<details id="before-you-start">
<summary><strong>Before you start</strong></summary>

### What this tool is for

- Organizing **your** materials for a **potential** landlord dispute.
- Reducing overwhelm so you can speak to a lawyer with clearer facts and dates.

### What it is not

- **Not legal advice.** It does not tell you what will win in court.
- **Not a substitute for a lawyer** for decisions about rights, filings, or strategy.

### What to have handy (optional)

- Lease or rental agreement (if you have it).
- Photos, messages, emails, or letters related to the problem.
- Rough dates when important things happened.

You can start empty and add items over time.

</details>

---

<details id="set-up-your-first-case">
<summary><strong>Set up your first case</strong></summary>

1. **Open the app** using the method described in the project README (for example, local web UI during development).
2. **Create or select a case** so your work is grouped in one place.
3. **Name it something you will recognize** (for example, building address + year).

<details>
<summary>Optional: set expectations</summary>

- You do not need a perfect narrative on day one.
- Small, regular updates beat one huge session.

</details>

</details>

---

<details id="add-documents-and-notes">
<summary><strong>Add documents and notes</strong></summary>

### Add what you already have

1. **Upload or attach** scans, PDFs, or exports where the app supports it.
2. **Add a short label** so you can find the file later (for example, “Notice to quit — March”).

### Capture facts, not arguments

- Prefer **who, what, when, where** over long stories at first.
- You can refine wording later.

<details>
<summary>Tips for messy situations</summary>

- If you are not sure of a date, **approximate** and mark it as approximate in your notes.
- **Screenshots** of texts or portals are fine as placeholders until you export cleaner copies.

</details>

</details>

---

<details id="build-your-timeline">
<summary><strong>Build your timeline</strong></summary>

1. **List events in order** (oldest to newest is usually easiest).
2. **Tie events to evidence** when possible (which message, which photo, which notice).
3. **Note gaps** (“missing exact date for repair request”) so you can fill them later.

<details>
<summary>Why the timeline matters</summary>

Lawyers and mediators often work from **chronology**. A clear timeline turns a pile of papers into a story they can scan quickly.

</details>

</details>

<details id="review-issues-and-next-steps">
<summary><strong>Review issues and next steps</strong></summary>

1. **Skim any summaries or issue lists** the app surfaces from your materials.
2. **Mark what feels uncertain** in your own notes so you can ask a lawyer.
3. **Avoid deciding legal outcomes yourself** — use the app to **organize questions**, not to “prove” a result.

<details>
<summary>Example questions for counsel</summary>

- “Does this notice meet statutory requirements in my jurisdiction?”
- “What deadlines apply if I want to respond or challenge this?”
- “Which of these events matter most for my situation?”

</details>

</details>

<details id="prepare-for-a-lawyer">
<summary><strong>Prepare for a lawyer</strong></summary>

1. **Export or compile** what the app provides (check the README for current export options).
2. **Bring your timeline** and **key documents** in the order you want to walk through.
3. **Write a one-page “top three concerns”** list so the first meeting stays focused.

<details>
<summary>First meeting checklist</summary>

- [ ] Timeline (even if incomplete)
- [ ] Lease or latest rental terms (if any)
- [ ] Notices or letters you received or sent
- [ ] Photos or inspection records (if relevant)
- [ ] List of questions you do not want to forget

</details>

</details>

---

<details id="privacy-and-data">
<summary><strong>Privacy and data</strong></summary>

- Treat exported files like **sensitive personal data**.
- **Store backups** where you control access (your device, encrypted storage, or as advised by counsel).
- **Review the product’s privacy notes** in the main README or design docs for how data is handled in your build.

</details>

<details id="if-you-get-stuck">
<summary><strong>If you get stuck</strong></summary>

- **Pause and capture one fact** instead of trying to finish everything at once.
- **Re-read the Quick start** in the root [`README.md`](../README.md).
- **Check documentation** under [`docs/`](../) for specs and implementation status if something in the UI does not match this guide yet.

</details>
