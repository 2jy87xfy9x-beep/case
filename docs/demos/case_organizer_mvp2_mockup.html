⚑  Potential Claims Identified
Retaliatory Rent Increase — CA Civil Code §1942.5
A rent increase issued after a tenant exercises habitability rights (repair request) may constitute retaliation. Burden shifts to landlord to prove a non-retaliatory reason.

Breach of Implied Warranty of Habitability — CA Civil Code §1941
Landlord's failure to repair ceiling water damage within a reasonable time may constitute breach.
